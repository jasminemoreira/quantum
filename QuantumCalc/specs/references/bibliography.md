## Representação exata / álgebra ciclotômica
- **Giles & Selinger**, "Exact synthesis of multiqubit Clifford+T circuits",
  Phys. Rev. A 87, 032332 — arXiv:1212.0506. *Anel D[ω]/ℤ[ω]/ℤ[1/√2,i],
  ω=e^{iπ/4}=(1+i)/√2, ω²=i, ω⁴=−1, conjugação ω†=−ω³, norma, expoente de
  denominador.* https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.0506
- **Kliuchnikov, Maslov & Mosca**, "Fast and efficient exact synthesis of single
  qubit unitaries generated by Clifford and T gates" — arXiv:1206.5236.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.5236
- **Forest, Gosset, Kliuchnikov & McKinnon**, "Exact synthesis over
  Clifford-cyclotomic gate sets", J. Math. Phys. 56, 082201 — arXiv:1501.04944.
  *ℤ[1/2, ζ_n] para rotações π/n.* https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.04944
- **Amy, Glaudell, Ross et al.**, "Exact Synthesis of Multiqubit
  Clifford-Cyclotomic Circuits" — arXiv:2311.07741. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07741
- **Ross & Selinger**, "Optimal ancilla-free Clifford+T approximation of
  z-rotations", QIC 16 — arXiv:1403.2975. *Rotações arbitrárias NÃO são exatas;
  T-count 3log₂(1/ε)+O(log log(1/ε)).* https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.2975
- **Giles & Selinger**, "Remarks on Matsumoto–Amano normal form" — arXiv:1312.6584.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6584
- **Dawson & Nielsen**, "The Solovay-Kitaev algorithm" — arXiv:quant-ph/0505030;
  Wikipedia "Solovay–Kitaev theorem". https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505030 |
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solovay%E2%80%93Kitaev_theorem
- **Dehaene & De Moor**, "Clifford group, stabilizer states…" — arXiv:quant-ph/0304125.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0304125
- Wikipedia, "Cyclotomic field" (anel de inteiros de ℚ(ζ_n) é ℤ[ζ_n]; φ(8)=4).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclotomic_field
